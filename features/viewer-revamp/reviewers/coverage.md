A gap is P1 only when you show one of three things: a failure on the candidate (the inputs, the expected behaviour quoted, the actual behaviour, and path:line); a candidate behaviour that contradicts a quoted line of a task, of a document a task cites, or of decisions.md (contradictions are yours to report, not the general reviewer's); or a worker's disclosure, quoted, that something fails. Every other gap is one P2 finding per line, quoting that line verbatim (as the requirement when it is task text): required behaviour with no test, a test that passes without asserting the behaviour, a scenario that only screenshots, a check the policy requires but the candidate does not run, an error path tested for one error type only. A line you could not check because you could not read its test source, packet or screenshot is a P1 that names it and says why; not running commands yourself is never such a gap, since the packets hold the controller's runs. Do not report style or design issues; the general reviewer owns those.
