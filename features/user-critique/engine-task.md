# Engine worker: the critique reviewer (PRD_USER_CRITIQUE)

## Goal

A feature.json 2.9.0 feature may declare `critique` (`lanes`, `skills`, `timeout_minutes`) with a policy 1.4.0 `critique_app`. Its automatic run's review step then has one more reviewer, `critique`, run as a controller-owned headless `claude --print --json-schema` job (PRD 4.4, revised after design challenge attempt 1; decisions.md [L1]): the controller gives it a disposable worktree of the frozen candidate with the policy `setup` run, starts the app there on free ports under a browser-queue slot, and runs the job with `Bash`, the pinned skills and the `builtin:critique` brief. The job uses the app as a user; the controller translates its structured output into a reviewer decision (PRD 4.5) whose open P1 findings block the review and feed the fix loop exactly as another reviewer's do, on attack runs too (PRD 4.6). The export (1.11.0) carries each critique finding's steps and screenshots (PRD Appendix A), which the `viewer` lane renders. This lane runs alone, before the `viewer` lane's run (PRD 4.8): its contract, examples and export are the viewer's base. Every feature before 2.9.0, and a 2.9.0 one without `critique`, launches byte for byte as today. `docs/PRD_USER_CRITIQUE.md` sections 3 to 6 are the specification; `features/user-critique/decisions.md` records the operator's decisions.
